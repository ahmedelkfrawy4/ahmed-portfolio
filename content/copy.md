## 6. Process — section header

> **How I work**
> Same four steps, every project. Predictable for you, room to think for me.

**Step 01 — Discover**
Interviews, audits, and the questions no one's asked yet. We find the real problem, not the requested one.

**Step 02 — Define**
A clear brief, success metrics, and the smallest version that's worth shipping. No design until this is agreed.

**Step 03 — Design**
Wireframes → polished UI → prototypes. You see work weekly, not at the end.

**Step 04 — Deliver**
Pixel-perfect handoff, design tokens, and a doc your engineers will actually read. I stick around through the build.

## 13. Voice & tone — quick reference

- **First person, conversational.** Never "John is a designer who…"
- **Short sentences.** Cut adverbs. Trust nouns and verbs.
- **Numbers > adjectives.** *"38% lift"* beats *"significant improvement"*.
- **Specific > clever.** *"6-week sprint with a 2-eng team"* beats *"agile collaboration"*.
- **No buzzwords.** Banished list: *synergy, leverage (verb), passionate, robust, holistic, scalable solution, end-to-end, unicorn, ninja, rockstar, world-class, cutting-edge.*
- **Confident, not boastful.** Show the work, let it speak.
